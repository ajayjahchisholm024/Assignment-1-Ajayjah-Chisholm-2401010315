Feel the Vibe!


Explore the diverse genres of island music that will make you move and groove! From reggae to soca to dancehall. Our events feature a wide range of musical styles that capture the essence of island culture. Discover your favorite genre and immerse yourself in the vibrant sounds of the islands.


The artists are the people who bring life and vibes to our lives through the powerful sounds of reggae, the energetic beats of dancehall, and the infectious rhythms of soca. Their music brings people together, celebrates Caribbean culture, and showcases the talent and creativity that make our region unique. From legendary performers to modern hitmakers, these artists keep the Caribbean spirit alive through their music.


Genres


Reggae

Reggae is a popular Jamaican music genre that developed in the late 1960s. It is known for its steady rhythm, deep bass and distinctive offbeat sound. Reggae songs often focus on love, peace, unity, freedom and social issues.

Soca

Soca is a lively Caribbean music genre that originated in Trinidad and Tobago during the 1970s. It combines elements of calypso with Indian musical influences and energetic rhythms. Soca music is known for its fast beats, cheerful lyrics and strong connection to Carnival celebrations, dancing, and parties throughout the Caribbean.


Alison Hinds

Destra Garcia

Machel Montano

Bunji Garlin

Fay-Ann Lyons

Kes


Dancehall

Dancehall is a Jamaican music genre that emerged in the late 1970s. It is characterized by heavy electronic beats, rhythmic vocals, and catchy lyrics. Dancehall music often focuses on dancing, everyday life, relationships, and social experiences.
